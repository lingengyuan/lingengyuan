# Hugh Lin

I build local AI systems on Apple Silicon.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of:

- paper reproduction
- local LLM training and inference
- MLX / MPS performance experiments
- practical tooling for real workflows

I like taking ideas that look good on paper and pushing them until they either work on a Mac or fail for a clear reason.

## Featured work

- **[gpt2-from-scratch](https://github.com/lingengyuan/gpt2-from-scratch)**  
  Rebuilt the full GPT-2 Small lifecycle on a MacBook Air M5, from data processing to quantized inference.

- **[minimind-autoresearch](https://github.com/lingengyuan/minimind-autoresearch)**  
  Ran a full Chinese small-model training study on Apple Silicon and documented what actually improved convergence.

- **[qjl-mlx](https://github.com/lingengyuan/qjl-mlx)**  
  Brought QJL and TurboQuant to MLX as an Apple Silicon native implementation.

- **[docflow](https://github.com/lingengyuan/docflow)**  
  A fully local multi-format knowledge assistant focused on privacy-first workflows.

- **[git-dungeon](https://github.com/lingengyuan/git-dungeon)**  
  Turned Git history into a playable command-line game.

## What I care about

- local-first AI
- Apple Silicon as a serious ML platform
- experiments with real numbers, not just demos
- small tools with strong personality
